Kids League Learn, pass, smile

Floorball is easy to start and hard to put down. Kids learn stick skills, movement, and teamwork in a gym — no ice time required.

Ages

Programs

Placeholder groups: U10 (roughly 8–10), U12, and U14. Sessions mix stations, small-area games, and a short scrimmage. We keep scores light and coaching loud enough to hear from the bleachers.

Parents stay in the gym. Loaner sticks are on the rack by the door. Indoor shoes only — no outdoor treads on the court.
