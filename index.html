About the club

Floorball in wine country

Sonoma County Floorball Club brings a fast, indoor stick-and-ball sport to gyms from Santa Rosa to Petaluma. Floorball plays a lot like floor hockey — a lightweight plastic ball, composite sticks, and little-to-no contact — so new players can jump in quickly.

Since 2023 we have run adult league nights, a kids program, open practices, and weekend tournaments. Whether you grew up with innebandy, found us after hockey, or have never held a stick, there is a roster waiting.
